Introduction

The world, people, tribes, seasonal arc and campaign structure

  • Campaign

Welcome to Eachtra midst the Sidhe, a campaign across the wild lands of Irgaelia, steeped in myth, magic and the turning of the seasons. The manuscript describes a world of eight gods, sacred groves, ancient stones and Sidhe courts whose conflicts reach into the mortal realm.

The campaign begins at Samhain and follows a seasonal structure. Morrigan manipulates Dullahan and the Wild Hunt, an unnatural winter spreads through Irgaelia, the Heart of Winter must be destroyed, the Fomorians invade after the thaw, and the heroes eventually enter the Otherworld to free the Summer Queen before confronting the forces behind the cycle.

The introduction also establishes the peoples of Irgaelia, its eight tribes and their patron spirits, and provides Irgaelic names for characters.
