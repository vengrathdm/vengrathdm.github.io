Running the Adventure

Villain guidance and campaign adjustments

  • Campaign

The manuscript's Running the Adventure section is intentionally concise in the supplied draft. It identifies guidance for playing the principal villains — Dullahan, Cailleach, Balor, the Autumn King and Morrigan — followed by advice on adjusting the campaign.

The source states that the campaign is written with a four-character adventuring party in mind.
