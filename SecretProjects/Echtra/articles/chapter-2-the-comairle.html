Chapter 2: The Comairle

Road to Comairle, Oldwood, the town and the Blind Eye

  • Adventure Chapter

After Dullahan's prophecy, the characters are tasked with warning the other tribes and reaching Comairle. The road south is deliberately filled with challenges that can be solved, bypassed or ignored while the party presses toward the gathering.

The route passes through Oldwood and Ravenpass before reaching the town of Comairle, whose locations, encounters and the Tower of the Blind Eye form the chapter's main exploration material.
