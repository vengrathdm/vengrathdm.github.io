Chapter 1: Samhain Festival

Cúilfeorna, Samhain rites and the arrival of Dullahan

  • Adventure Chapter

The campaign opens on the last day of the Old Year at the Samhain Festival in Cúilfeorna, Irgaelia's northernmost permanent settlement. The characters are given time to meet one another, feast, tell fortunes and participate in the rites before Dullahan arrives.

Dullahan, secretly sent by Morrigan, is destined to take a life and announce the coming of the Wild Hunt. The chapter's atmosphere is therefore built around a festival whose ordinary rituals become the threshold to the campaign's supernatural crisis.
