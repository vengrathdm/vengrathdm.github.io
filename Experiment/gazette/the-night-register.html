Who Was Seen at the Winter Assembly?

An invitation without a sender. A guest list with one name too many. A night that the official record says never happened.

By M. AvenFrom our own correspondentsHeartwell edition

Forty-two names in the published copy

The annual Winter Assembly concluded before dawn under circumstances that the Palace Press Office has called entirely ordinary. The guest register, released yesterday, records forty-two attendees and no incidents. This newspaper has obtained a copy of an earlier list containing a forty-third name, written in a hand not recognised by the clerks.

The discrepancy would be unremarkable were it not for the testimony of two musicians and a kitchen porter. Each recalls a place at the high table that remained untouched throughout the evening. Each describes the same black glass, the same red napkin, and a chair turned slightly away from the room.

A palace spokesperson dismissed the accounts as an attempt to embarrass the household. The musicians have since withdrawn their statements. The porter cannot be located.

The empty chair had been set for someone who arrived before the invitation was written.

The photograph in the envelope

A sealed envelope was delivered to the Gazette this morning. It contained a pressed flower, a strip of photographic negative, and a note bearing only the words: COUNT AGAIN. The negative appears to show the Assembly Hall from the musicians' gallery. In the reflection of the far window, a figure can be seen standing behind the high table.

Our photographic editor has not been able to identify the figure. The Palace Press Office has requested the original negative and threatened legal action if it is reproduced. We have declined to surrender it.

Readers who possess a copy of the original guest list are invited to contact the newsroom. Correspondence will be treated in confidence.

From the editor's desk

The earlier register is held in a locked cabinet at the Palace. A copy delivered to this office bears a different seal from the one used on the published document.

Notice to readers
