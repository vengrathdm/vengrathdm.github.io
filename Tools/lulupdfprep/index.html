📏 Gutter Reference
Based on your page count, here are Lulu's recommended gutter additions for readability. These are applied to the inside margin (spine side) of each page.

Less than 60 pages: No extra gutter needed (0.5 in total inside margin)
61–150 pages: Add 0.125 in → 0.625 in total inside margin
151–400 pages: Add 0.5 in → 1 in total inside margin
401–600 pages: Add 0.625 in → 1.125 in total inside margin
Over 600 pages: Add 0.75 in → 1.25 in total inside margin

Note: Gutter adjustments require modifying your source document layout. This tool adjusts page dimensions and bleed but cannot reflow text content.
